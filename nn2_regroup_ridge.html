Concept

What is it and what are its parts?

What is it?

Regrouping means changing 10 of one unit into 1 of the next unit (10 ones → 1 ten), or 1 unit into 10 of the unit to its right (1 hundred → 10 tens). We regroup when a column has 10 or more in addition, or when the top digit is too small in subtraction.

Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract

How we learn it: hands, pictures, symbols
🧠 Psychologist Jerome Bruner showed that we understand ideas in three steps: first we do it with real objects (enactive), then we draw it (iconic), and finally we write it with numbers and symbols (symbolic).

Step 1 · Enactive✋ Concrete

Solve with base-ten blocks on a mat. Trade 10 ones for 1 ten (addition). Break 1 ten into 10 ones (subtraction).
✏️ Write it! Write the trade you made: “10 ones → 1 ten”.

Step 2 · Iconic🖍️ Pictorial

Draw a bar model or a quick picture (squares, lines and dots) to see the parts and the whole.
✏️ Write it! Draw the bar model for 5,000 − 2,368 and label the parts.

Step 3 · Symbolic🔣 Abstract

Write the algorithm in columns. Estimate first, then check with the inverse operation.

Strategies

Tools to solve it

Estimate first

Round each number, add or subtract, and keep the estimate to check your answer.

Line up the places

Ones under ones, tens under tens… Always start from the ones.

Regroup and write it

Write the regrouped 1 above the next column so you do not forget it.

Across zeros

5,000 → 4 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, 10 ones. Then subtract.

Check with the inverse

Check a subtraction with addition: difference + subtrahend = minuend.
