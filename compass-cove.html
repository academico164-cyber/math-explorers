Cartesian Plane

What is it?

The cartesian plane has two number lines that cross at the origin, letting us describe the exact position of any point.

Parts of a Coordinate

5➡X-coordinate (right)
3➡Y-coordinate (up)

🗺️ What is the coordinate plane?

The coordinate plane has two number lines that cross each other: a horizontal line called the x-axis, and a vertical line called the y-axis. The point where they cross is called the origin, and it is always (0,0).

✍️ Write these three words in your notebook: x-axis, y-axis, origin

📍 How to read a coordinate (x , y)

An ordered pair like (5 , 3) always has two numbers. Start at the origin. The first number tells you how many steps to move right along the x-axis. The second number tells you how many steps to move up along the y-axis.

✍️ Write in your notebook: "First = right (x), Second = up (y)"
